RIAN: So, what exactly is the Zig programming language? Basically, Zig is a modern programming language designed to give you a lot of control over how your programs run, without making things unnecessarily complicated. It’s mainly used for systems programming, like operating systems, tools, and software where performance really matters. Unlike some languages, Zig doesn’t have a hidden garbage collector cleaning up memory for you. Instead, you manage things more directly. It also focuses on being simple, predictable, and easy to build across different platforms. In other words, Zig gives you low-level control without making you fight the language every five minutes.
