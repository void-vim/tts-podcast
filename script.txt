RIAN: So, what exactly is an NFT?

BOB: NFT stands for Non-Fungible Token. It’s a unique digital asset recorded on a blockchain, which can prove who owns it and where it came from.

RIAN: So is an NFT just a digital picture?

BOB: Not necessarily. An NFT can represent artwork, music, collectibles, tickets, game items, memberships, or even ownership of something digital.

RIAN: But if I can right-click and save the picture, what am I actually buying?

BOB: Usually, you’re buying ownership of the NFT itself, not exclusive ownership of the image or its copyright. Anyone might be able to copy the image, but the blockchain records which wallet owns the specific NFT.

RIAN: Then why would anyone want one?

BOB: NFTs can provide verifiable digital ownership and scarcity. They can also be used in games, communities, events, and other applications where proving ownership matters.

RIAN: Are NFTs still valuable?

BOB: Some can be, but their prices are highly speculative. An NFT can become extremely valuable, or practically worthless, depending on demand.

RIAN: So, in short, an NFT is a blockchain-based token designed to represent something unique, giving digital assets a way to have verifiable ownership and scarcity.
